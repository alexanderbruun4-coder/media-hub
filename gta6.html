02 — Premise & cast

Story and characters

A Bonnie-and-Clyde crime story that starts with a job gone wrong and spirals into a conspiracy that runs the length of the state.

“Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them. But when an easy score goes wrong, they find themselves on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America, in the middle of a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida.” — Rockstar Games, official synopsis

The two leads

L

Playable protagonist

Lucia Caminos

Voiced by Manni L. Perez

The first female protagonist in a mainline Grand Theft Auto. Lucia comes out of Leonida Penitentiary at the start of the story, having taken the fall for a fight that was about protecting her family. She's on parole, out of patience, and looking for the one score that buys the life she was promised.

  • Latina lead; family roots run through Liberty City
  • Freshly released, on parole as the story opens
  • Shown handling melee brawls, dodges and struggle sequences
J

Playable protagonist

Jason Duval

Voiced by Dylan Rourke

A former soldier who grew up around hustlers and drifted back into the life after service. When we meet him he's running errands for local drug traffickers in the Keys, telling himself it's temporary. He wants an easy life; Leonida keeps sending the bill.

  • Ex-military; comfortable with guns, less so with the consequences
  • Based in the Leonida Keys before the story pulls him inland
  • Wears a V-Rock shirt — the only radio station teased so far

Supporting cast

Brian Heder

Old-school criminal running things out of the Keys, and Jason's way into the local trade.

Cal Hampton

Jason's friend and a committed conspiracy theorist — the kind who is occasionally, alarmingly, right.

Boobie Ike

Vice City businessman who turned street money into legitimate holdings, including a strip club and a studio.

Dre'Quan Priest

Music promoter working with Boobie Ike, building an artist roster out of Vice City's club scene.

Real Dimez

Rap duo Roxy and Bae-Bae, signed by Dre'Quan — the game's window into Leonida's music economy.

Raul Bautista

A career bank robber who pulls Jason and Lucia into the crew work the story is built around.

How the partnership works

You control Jason and Lucia interchangeably through most of the campaign. The romance between them is optional — you can keep the partnership strictly professional and still see the whole story — but the strength of their bond changes how certain scenes and events play out. Missions branch too: some let you choose whether to drive or to shoot while your partner takes the wheel. Reported from previews

04 — Systems

How it actually plays

Rockstar's 26-minute Extended Look and the press previews around it produced the first real detail on mechanics. Filter by what you care about.

Criminal Profile

Replaces Red Dead 2's Honor meter. It tracks what kind of criminal you are in free roam rather than sorting you into good and evil — consistent behaviour skews the profile in a direction rather than scoring you.

Reported

Six-star wanted level

The sixth star is back after GTA 5 capped out at five. Police build a picture of you from physical description, outfit and vehicle — change any of them and you change what they're looking for.

Reported

No cops on the minimap

Police no longer show as blips. You use the Focus mechanic to spot them, which turns every chase into a question of what you can actually see.

Reported

Focus

A held-input mode that highlights nearby objects, interactables and useful details in the environment — including police during a pursuit.

Reported

Slow-motion aiming

A Dead Eye–style bullet-time mode carries over from Red Dead Redemption 2, with target highlighting. Regular aiming is visibly smoother than GTA 5's.

Shown in Extended Look

Dodging and struggles

Melee gets a dedicated dodge. When a fight goes badly — Lucia is overpowered in one shown sequence — you fight out of it through timed prompts rather than losing control of the scene.

Shown in Extended Look

Limited weapon carrying

No bottomless weapon wheel. You carry two rifles and two pistols; anything else lives in a vehicle trunk or at a safehouse, so loadout becomes a decision you make before a job.

Reported

Robberies: speed vs. take

Robberies are built around the trade-off between grabbing everything and getting out clean. Locations have multiple exits, so escaping is a plan rather than a cutscene.

Reported

Chapter-based campaign

The story is structured in chapters across roughly 80 hours, with choices ranging from small talk to genuine turning points.

Reported

Optional romance, real consequences

Jason and Lucia's relationship can stay platonic without cutting content, but the strength of their bond changes how certain events and scenes play.

Reported

Mission freedom

Objectives allow more freedom in how you complete them and how you get away — including choosing whether you drive or shoot while your partner handles the other half.

Reported

NPCs you can actually talk to

A contextual interaction system lets you greet, taunt, provoke or defuse the people around you — the same verb set Red Dead 2 used, in a city that answers back.

Reported

600,000+ NPC animations

Against roughly 55,000 in GTA 5. Crowds are also individually art-directed rather than assembled purely by shuffling clothing and body parts procedurally.

Reported

The phone is a physical object

Your phone exists in the world in your character's hand rather than as a flat interface pasted over the screen.

Reported

30 fps on PS5

The Extended Look was captured entirely in-game on PlayStation 5, and previews clocked it at 30 fps. Rockstar has not detailed performance modes or PS5 Pro enhancements.

Reported

No first-person mode at launch

GTA 5's first-person view is reportedly not in GTA 6 at release. Rockstar has not commented either way.

Reported

Vehicles

  • Both protagonists build their own collection of personal vehicles, parked at whichever residence they're using.
  • Trunks are usable storage — stash spare weapons and a change of clothes for after a job.
  • Modding covers both performance and style: detailed interiors, rims, and full donk builds.
  • Shown in footage: classic convertibles, fan boats, vans, jet skis, helicopters and a proper racing circuit.
Reported from previews

Customization

  • Every clothing store and salon carries its own selection, so shopping across Leonida actually matters.
  • Your outfit feeds the wanted system — police describe what you're wearing.
  • Ultimate Edition weapons carry custom grips, engravings and scopes.
  • Character appearance options, hair and grooming are handled per-business rather than from one global menu.
Reported from previews

07 — What Rockstar has shown

Trailers, footage and music

Three official pieces of footage in three years, and 21 licensed songs used across them.

Dec 4, 2023

Trailer 1

Announced for December 5 and pushed out a day early after it leaked. It set a YouTube record for a non-music video with over 90 million views in 24 hours, and introduced Lucia, Jason and the state of Leonida.

♪ Tom Petty — “Love Is a Long Road”

May 6, 2025

Trailer 2

Arrived days after the first delay. Longer, warmer, and far more focused on the pair as a couple — plus the first real look at Port Gellhorn, the Keys and the wetlands.

♪ Jay Ferguson “Thunder Island” · Zenglen “Child Support” · Wang Chung “Everybody Have Fun Tonight” · Tammy Wynette “Talkin' to Myself Again” · The Pointer Sisters “Hot Together”

Aug 27, 2026

GTA 6: An Extended Look

A 26-minute gameplay showcase that premiered on Netflix and hit Rockstar's YouTube channel and website roughly six hours later. Captured entirely in-game on PS5, it remains by far the most substantial official look at the game, and it's where nearly every mechanic on this page comes from.

♪ 14 further songs, spanning Def Leppard and Phil Collins to Birdman, Lil Wayne, Kodak Black and Sexyy Red

About the soundtrack

Rockstar has not said that the 21 tracks used in its marketing will appear on GTA 6's radio. Some may end up as mission or environmental music instead. V-Rock — the Vice City rock station — is the only station teased so far, and only via a t-shirt Jason wears in footage. No radio lineup, DJ or host has been announced.

08 — How we got here

Development timeline

From a one-line confirmation buried in a community update to the longest pre-release cycle in the series' history.

  1. Feb 4, 2022

    Rockstar confirms development

    A GTA 5 community update ends with the line that work on the next entry is “well underway.” That's the entire announcement.

  2. Sept 2022

    The leak

    More than 90 clips of in-development footage are posted online in one of the largest leaks in game history. Rockstar confirms the intrusion and says it doesn't expect long-term disruption.

  3. Dec 4, 2023

    Trailer 1 — release window: 2025

    Released a day early after leaking. Take-Two later clarifies the window as fall 2025.

  4. May 2, 2025

    Delay #1 — moved to May 26, 2026

    The first firm date, and the first slip out of 2025.

  5. May 6, 2025

    Trailer 2

    Arrives days after the delay, breaking the record Trailer 1 set.

  6. Nov 2025

    Delay #2 — moved to November 19, 2026

    The second and, so far, final slip. Two delays total, despite persistent claims of three.

  7. Jun 25, 2026

    Pre-orders open

    Standard at $79.99 and Ultimate at $99.99, both with the Vintage Vice City Pack.

  8. Aug 7, 2026

    Take-Two reaffirms the date

    Quarterly results restate November 19, 2026 with no change to the plan.

  9. Aug 27, 2026

    An Extended Look premieres on Netflix

    26 minutes of PS5 gameplay, followed by press previews that produce well over 150 new details.

  10. Nov 19, 2026

    Launch

    PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

09 — The honest part

Still unknown

Everything below is genuinely unanswered. If a site tells you otherwise, it's guessing.

The PC version

No release date, no storefront, no system requirements — nothing announced at all. GTA 5 took roughly 19 months to reach PC after its console launch, but that's precedent, not a promise.

Online

GTA 6 ships as a single-player game. Rockstar has announced no structure, features or date for any successor to GTA Online, and hasn't said what happens to GTA Online itself.

Cross-play

Moot at launch with no multiplayer, and unaddressed for whatever comes after.

Map size

Rockstar has published no figure. The “twice Los Santos” and “three times RDR2” comparisons come from press impressions.

Radio lineup

One station teased on a t-shirt. No confirmed station list, hosts, DJs or tracklist.

First-person mode

Reportedly absent at launch, but Rockstar has never confirmed or denied it.

Physical collector's edition

None announced, and Rockstar has signalled no plans for one. Every leaked price tier for it was speculation.

Post-launch plans

No story expansions, DLC or roadmap have been announced.
